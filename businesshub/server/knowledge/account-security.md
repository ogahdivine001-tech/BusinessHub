## How do I reset my password?
On the login page choose **Forgot password** and enter your email address. We email you a reset link that works for {{limits.resetLinkMinutes}} minutes. The reply is the same whether or not the address has an account, so nobody can use the form to find out who has one.

## How do I confirm my email address?
We email you a confirmation link when you sign up. It works once and expires after {{limits.verifyEmailHours}} hours. You can ask for a new one in **Settings → Profile** (up to {{limits.verifyRequestsPerHour}} requests per hour).

## How long do I stay logged in?
Up to {{limits.sessionDays}} days on a device, unless you log out.

## How do I keep my account safe?
Use a password you do not use anywhere else, at least {{limits.passwordMinChars}} characters long. Never share your password with anyone, and never type it into the assistant chat. Be careful with emails that ask you to log in through a link you did not expect.
