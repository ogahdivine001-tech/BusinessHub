## Why can't I create an invoice or receipt?
Either your plan does not include them (Free on newer accounts does not), or you have reached your monthly limit. Open **Settings → Subscription** to see your plan, what it includes and your usage this month. If your plan has just ended it moves to Free, and creating new invoices pauses until you renew. Your existing invoices stay available.

## Why can't I add more products?
You have reached your plan's product limit ({{plans.free.products}} on Free, {{plans.starter.products}} on Starter, {{plans.pro.products}} on Pro for new plans). Delete products you no longer sell or upgrade. Your existing products are never deleted for you.

## I paid but my plan did not change
Wait a minute and refresh Settings → Subscription: we confirm every payment with Paystack before changing a plan. If it still has not changed, contact us from the Contact page and include the Paystack payment reference from your receipt email, so we can check it.

## My plan shows Free but I paid last month
Each payment buys {{billing.periodDays}} days and does not renew by itself. Check the end date in Settings → Subscription and renew to restore your tools. Your data is safe in the meantime.

## The verification email did not arrive
Check your spam folder, then request another from **Settings → Profile**. You can request up to {{limits.verifyRequestsPerHour}} per hour. Links work once and expire after {{limits.verifyEmailHours}} hours.

## A customer says my invoice link is invalid or expired
Invoice and receipt links last {{links.pdfDays}} days. Open the invoice in your dashboard, use **Download PDF**, and send the new link.

## My verification badge is not showing
It only appears after an administrator approves your request. Check **Verification** in your dashboard for the current status. A pending request is not shown publicly.

## A review I wrote is not visible
If a moderator hid it you will see it marked "hidden" under your own review on that business. Hidden reviews are not shown to the public or counted in ratings.

## My business does not appear in search
Make sure your page is published and that your name, category, city and description are filled in. New businesses with no reviews are listed after reviewed ones when sorting by rating, but still appear in normal search and under Newest.

## My images will not upload
Use a PNG, JPG, WebP, GIF or AVIF photo under {{limits.imageMb}} MB.

## The AI says it is unavailable
The AI service may be down for a moment, or your plan may not include that tool, or you may have used this month's allowance. The message tells you which. The Help Center is always available.
