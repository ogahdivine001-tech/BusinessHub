## How do I add a product?
Go to **Products** and choose **Add Product**. Enter a name, description (up to {{limits.productDescriptionChars}} characters), price, optional discount percentage, category, stock quantity and optional SKU, then add photos and save. The product appears on your storefront straight away.

## What photos can I upload?
PNG, JPG, WebP, GIF or AVIF images up to {{limits.imageMb}} MB each. Other file types are rejected for safety.

## How many products can I add?
It depends on your plan: Free allows {{plans.free.products}}, Starter allows {{plans.starter.products}} and Pro allows {{plans.pro.products}}. The limit counts all your products at once, not new products per month. Accounts created before the current plan terms keep their earlier limits, for example {{legacy.starter.products}} products on an older Starter plan.

## What happens if my plan ends and I have more products than Free allows?
Nothing is deleted. Your products stay and stay visible. You just cannot add new ones until you are under the limit or upgrade again.

## How does stock work?
Each product has a stock quantity. When a customer orders from your storefront the quantity goes down automatically, and you get a notification when stock runs low. A product with zero stock shows as out of stock.
