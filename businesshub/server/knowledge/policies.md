## What is BusinessHub's role?
BusinessHub is a platform that gives businesses an online storefront and tools. Each business is responsible for its own products, prices, delivery and customer service. We are not a party to the sale between a business and its customers.

## Does BusinessHub handle money between customers and businesses?
No. We do not collect or hold payments for customer orders. The only payments taken through BusinessHub are for BusinessHub plans (and the verification fee, if one applies), processed by Paystack.

## What can BusinessHub remove?
Administrators can hide reviews that break the rules, remove verification badges, unpublish a business page and deactivate an account when it breaks our Terms. Rejections and removals of badges come with a reason, and every administrator action is recorded.

## Are refunds available?
