## How do I say whether I offer delivery?
Open **My Business** and find the **Delivery** section. Choose **Delivery available**, **Delivery not available**, or leave it as **Not specified**. If you offer delivery you can add the areas you deliver to, your delivery terms and how customers should arrange it with you. Save to publish the change.

## What does BusinessHub do about delivery?
BusinessHub only displays what you tell customers. We do not arrange deliveries, set delivery fees or promise delivery times. Agree prices and timing directly with your customer. Customers can filter the Discover page to show businesses that offer delivery.

## Why does my page not mention delivery?
Delivery is shown only when you have chosen "Delivery available" or "Delivery not available". Businesses that have not set it show nothing, so customers are never told something you did not say.
