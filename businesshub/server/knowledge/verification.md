## What does the verified badge mean?
The badge tells customers that a BusinessHub administrator has reviewed the details a business supplied. It appears on the storefront, in Discover results and on business cards. It is not a guarantee of product quality or service.

## How do I get verified?
Open **Verification** in your dashboard and send a request with your registered business name (required) and, if you have them, your registration number (such as CAC), a website or social page, and any notes that help us confirm your business. Our team reviews each request by hand and you are notified of the decision.

## Can I buy a badge?
No. A badge is never given just because someone paid. Every request is reviewed by an administrator, and the badge can be refused or removed.

## What if my request is rejected?
You see the reason in your Verification page and you can apply again once the issue is fixed.

## Can a badge be removed?
Yes. An administrator can remove it, with a recorded reason, for example if the details turn out to be wrong. You are notified, and you can apply again.

## The statuses explained
**Not verified**: you have not asked. **Pending**: your request is waiting for review. **Verified**: approved. **Rejected**: not approved, see the reason. **Revoked**: a previous badge was removed.
