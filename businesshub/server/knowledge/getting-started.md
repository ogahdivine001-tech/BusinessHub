BusinessHub helps you put your business online: a storefront page customers can visit, a product catalogue, orders, invoices and receipts, and a way to be found by new customers.

## How do I create an account?
Choose **Sign up**, then pick the kind of account you need: **I want to sell** (to create a business page) or **I'm a customer** (to find businesses, follow them and leave reviews). Enter your full name, email address, phone number and a password of at least {{limits.passwordMinChars}} characters. After you sign up we send you an email to confirm your address. You can use BusinessHub before confirming it, but confirming is needed for some actions, such as writing reviews.

## What happens after I sign up?
You are guided through setting up your business page in six short steps: business name, category, description, contact details, location and style. When you finish, your storefront is live and you land on your dashboard.

## Is there a free trial?
Yes. When you set up your business page you get {{trial.days}} days of Pro features free, with no card needed. After the trial your account moves to the Free plan unless you choose a paid plan. Nothing is deleted when the trial ends.

## What is the difference between a seller account and a customer account?
A **seller account** is for running a business: you set up a business page, add products, receive orders and, on paid plans, create invoices and receipts. A **customer account** is for people who shop: you can follow businesses, write reviews, see the businesses you follow and the reviews you wrote, and get notifications. Customer accounts are free and do not need a business page. You do not need any account to browse businesses or to place an order.

## Can I switch from a customer account to a seller account?
Yes. Open **My account** and choose **Start selling on BusinessHub**. Your account becomes a seller account and you are taken through the business setup. Your follows and reviews are kept. The switch is one-way.

## Can a seller also follow businesses and write reviews?
Yes. Sellers can follow other businesses and review them like anyone else (but never their own). Open **Following & reviews** in your dashboard menu to see them.

## Where do I find things?
Sellers have a dashboard with My Business (your page and settings), Products, Orders, Customers, Invoices, Receipts, Analytics, AI Assistant, Marketing, Verification, Notifications and Settings.
