Every business has a public storefront that shows your details and products, and lets customers contact you and place orders.

## What does my storefront show?
Your logo and cover image, business name and description, contact details and location, opening hours (if you add them), your delivery information (if you set it), your products, and customer reviews with your average rating and follower count. A verification badge appears only if BusinessHub has verified your business.

## How do I edit my business page?
Open **My Business** in your dashboard. You can change your name, description, logo, cover image, contact details, location, opening hours, delivery settings and style, then save.

## Can I change how my page looks?
Yes. You can choose a theme. Classic, Modern and Minimal are available to everyone. The Bold theme is {{plans.pro.premiumThemes}} on the Pro plan only.

## Can I remove the "Powered by BusinessHub" branding?
Removing it is {{plans.starter.brandingRemoval}} on the Starter and Pro plans and {{plans.free.brandingRemoval}} on Free.

## How do customers find my page?
They can open your storefront link, or find you on the Discover page by searching for your name, category, city or products. Share your storefront link on WhatsApp, Instagram and anywhere else your customers are.
