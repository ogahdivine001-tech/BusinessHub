## How do customers place orders?
On your storefront a customer picks a product and a quantity (1 to {{limits.orderMaxQuantity}}), enters their name and phone number, and sends the order. It is saved in your **Orders** page as Pending and unpaid. If you have added a WhatsApp number, the storefront also opens WhatsApp with the order details filled in, so you can confirm directly with the customer. You can also create orders yourself from the Orders page.

## Does BusinessHub collect payments for my orders?
No. BusinessHub does not take payments from your customers or hold your money. You and your customer agree how to pay and how the order is delivered, for example by bank transfer or cash on delivery. You can mark the order as paid yourself once you have been paid.

## What are the order statuses?
An order can be Pending, Processing, Completed or Cancelled, and you can mark it Paid or Unpaid. These are yours to update; they help you keep track and are not checked by BusinessHub.

## Where do my customers come from?
When someone orders from your storefront we create a customer record from their name and phone number, or reuse the existing one. Open **Customers** to see each customer and their order history.
