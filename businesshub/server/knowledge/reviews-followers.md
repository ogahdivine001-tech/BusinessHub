## How do customers review a business?
On a storefront, a logged-in customer chooses 1 to 5 stars and can add a written comment of up to {{limits.reviewMaxChars}} characters. {{reviews.emailRule}} Each person can leave one review per business, and can edit or delete it later. You cannot review your own business.

## Do reviews prove the customer bought something?
No. BusinessHub does not check that a reviewer purchased anything, so reviews are opinions from BusinessHub users, not confirmed purchases.

## How is a business's rating worked out?
It is the average of its published reviews. A business with no reviews shows "No reviews yet" instead of a rating. Hidden reviews are not counted.

## Can reviews be removed?
Reviews that break the rules, such as abusive language, can be hidden by a BusinessHub moderator, who must give a reason. The rating is recalculated without them. The reviewer can still see their own hidden review marked as hidden, but cannot restore it.

## Can businesses delete bad reviews?
No. A business cannot remove or edit reviews about itself. If you think a review breaks the rules, contact us from the Contact page.

## How does following work?
A logged-in customer can follow a business and unfollow it later. The follower count is the real number of people currently following. You cannot follow your own business.

## Where can I see the businesses I follow and the reviews I wrote?
Open **My account**. **Following** lists the businesses you follow, with a way to unfollow, and **My reviews** lists every review you have written, including any that a moderator hid. Sellers find the same pages under **Following & reviews** in their dashboard menu.
