## What plans are there and what do they include?
**Free** has {{plans.free.products}} products and your business page, with basic analytics ({{plans.free.analytics}}). Invoices, receipts and AI are not included.

**Starter** ({{billing.starterPrice}} for {{billing.periodDays}} days) allows {{plans.starter.products}} products, invoices ({{plans.starter.invoices}}), receipts ({{plans.starter.receipts}}), AI business chat ({{plans.starter.aiChat}}), AI help writing invoices and receipts ({{plans.starter.aiDocuments}}), custom branding and analytics ({{plans.starter.analytics}}). AI marketing tools are {{plans.starter.aiMarketing}}.

**Pro** ({{billing.proPrice}} for {{billing.periodDays}} days) has {{plans.pro.products}} products, everything in Starter, the AI marketing tools and premium themes. AI has no monthly cap on Pro, but requests are limited to {{limits.aiPerMinute}} per minute to keep the service fast for everyone (fair use).

## How does paying work?
You pay with Paystack on the Settings → Subscription page. Each payment buys {{billing.periodDays}} days. Payments are not automatic: nothing is charged again until you choose to renew.

## How do I renew?
Open **Settings → Subscription** and choose **Renew**. If your plan is still running, the {{billing.periodDays}} days are added to the end of your current period, so renewing early never wastes days. You see a banner in your dashboard when your plan has 3 days left.

## What if I upgrade from Starter to Pro?
Upgrading starts a new {{billing.periodDays}}-day Pro period from today. Unused Starter days are not credited towards Pro. While Pro is running you cannot buy Starter over it; you can choose Starter after Pro ends.

## What happens when my plan ends?
Your account moves to the Free plan. Nothing is deleted: your products, invoices and receipts stay safe. Tools that Free does not include, such as creating new invoices or using AI, pause until you renew.

## How does the free trial work?
New businesses get {{trial.days}} days of Pro features, free and without a card. After that the account is on Free unless you pay for a plan.

## I signed up before the plans changed. What do I get?
Accounts created under earlier plan terms keep those terms. For example an older Starter plan allows {{legacy.starter.products}} products, and an older Free plan includes {{legacy.free.invoices}} invoices and {{legacy.free.aiShared}}. Your Settings → Subscription page always shows the limits that apply to you.

## Where can I see my usage?
**Settings → Subscription** shows your products, invoices, receipts and AI use against your plan limits for this month.
