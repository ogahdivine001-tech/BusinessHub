## Can I accept payments through BusinessHub?
Not for customer orders. BusinessHub records the order, and you agree payment and delivery directly with your customer. Paystack is used only for BusinessHub plan payments and the verification fee.

## Is BusinessHub free?
You can start free with {{plans.free.products}} products and your own business page, and every new business gets a {{trial.days}}-day Pro trial. Paid plans are {{billing.starterPrice}} (Starter) or {{billing.proPrice}} (Pro) for {{billing.periodDays}} days.

## Do I need a card to start?
No. The free trial and the Free plan do not need a card.

## Can I cancel?
Plans are not automatic, so there is nothing to cancel. If you do not renew, your account moves to Free when the {{billing.periodDays}} days end.

## Is my data deleted if my plan ends?
No. Your products, invoices, receipts and customers stay. Some tools pause until you renew.

## How do I contact BusinessHub?
Use the [Contact page](/contact).

## Do customers need an account?
No. Anyone can browse businesses and place an order without an account. A free customer account lets you follow businesses, write reviews and keep track of both.
