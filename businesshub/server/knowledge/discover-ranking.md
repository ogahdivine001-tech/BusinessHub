## How do customers search for businesses?
The Discover page searches business names, categories, descriptions, cities and the products businesses sell. Customers can filter by category, city, delivery available, verified only and minimum rating, and sort by best match, top rated, most followed, newest or name.

## How is "Top rated" decided?
Businesses need at least {{reviews.minForTopRated}} reviews to be listed as top rated. Within that group a fair score is used that takes into account how many reviews a business has, so a single 5-star review cannot outrank an established business. Businesses with fewer reviews are listed after those with enough reviews, and businesses with no reviews come last when sorting by rating.

## What is "Popular"?
Popular businesses are the most followed. It is not a sales ranking: BusinessHub does not show "top selling" lists because order records are entered by the businesses themselves.

## How can I appear more often in search?
Fill in your description, category and city, add products with clear names, keep your page up to date, and encourage happy customers to follow you and leave reviews. We never sell ranking positions.

## Why can't customers find my business?
Check that your business page is published, your name, category and city are filled in, and your products have clear names. See the troubleshooting article for more.
