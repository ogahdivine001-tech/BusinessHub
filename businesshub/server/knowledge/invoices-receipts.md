## How do I create an invoice?
Go to **Invoices** and choose **Create Invoice**. Pick a customer, add items (description, quantity and price), and optionally a discount, tax, due date and notes. BusinessHub calculates the totals and gives the invoice a unique number. You can mark it paid or unpaid later.

## How do I create a receipt?
Go to **Receipts**, choose **Generate Receipt**, pick the customer, add the items and choose how they paid (cash, transfer, card, Paystack or other). A receipt is your own record of a payment you received. BusinessHub does not check that the payment actually happened, so only create a receipt once you have been paid.

## How do I send an invoice or receipt to a customer?
Open the document and use **Download PDF**. The PDF opens from a secure link that you can copy and send. The link works for {{links.pdfDays}} days. Each time you open your invoice or receipt list you get a fresh link, so the business is never locked out. A customer holding an old link will see that it has expired and can ask you for a new one.

## Can I put my bank details on invoices?
Yes. Add your bank details under **My Business** and they appear on your invoices so customers know where to pay.

## What happens to my invoices if my plan ends?
They are safe. You can still view and download every invoice and receipt you made. Only creating new ones is paused until you renew.

## Can AI help write invoice notes?
On Starter and Pro, the invoice form has a "Draft notes with AI" button that suggests wording for the notes. You always review and edit it before saving. See the AI tools article for limits.
