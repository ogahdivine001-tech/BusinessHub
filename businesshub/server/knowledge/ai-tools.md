## What AI features does BusinessHub have?
**AI business chat** is the BusinessHub Assistant: it answers questions about using BusinessHub and gives general business and marketing ideas. **AI marketing tools** generate captions, advertisements, product descriptions, business descriptions, marketing ideas and product names from details you enter. **AI invoice writing** drafts invoice notes. Everything the AI writes is a suggestion that you review and edit; nothing is published or sent automatically.

## Which plans include AI?
AI business chat: Free {{plans.free.aiChat}}, Starter {{plans.starter.aiChat}}, Pro {{plans.pro.aiChat}}. AI marketing tools: Free {{plans.free.aiMarketing}}, Starter {{plans.starter.aiMarketing}}, Pro {{plans.pro.aiMarketing}}. AI help writing invoices and receipts: Free {{plans.free.aiDocuments}}, Starter {{plans.starter.aiDocuments}}, Pro {{plans.pro.aiDocuments}}. Accounts on earlier terms share one monthly allowance across all AI tools: {{legacy.free.aiShared}} on Free and {{legacy.starter.aiShared}} on Starter.

## Is the AI always right?
No. AI can make mistakes. Check prices, claims and facts before you use anything it writes. For account, billing or policy questions rely on the Help Center and your Settings pages.

## What can the assistant not do?
It cannot change your settings, take payments, or see other people's accounts, customers or orders. It only knows the BusinessHub help content and a short summary of your own plan and usage. Please do not paste passwords or card numbers into the chat.

## What does the assistant use?
BusinessHub provides the assistant, so you do not need your own AI key. It uses a third-party language model through BusinessHub. If the AI service is unavailable the assistant still shows you the most relevant help articles.

## Why does it say AI is unavailable?
The AI service may be temporarily down, or it may not be switched on for this installation. Try again in a moment. If you reached your monthly limit you will see a message saying so.
